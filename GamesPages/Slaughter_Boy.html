Slaughter Boy

An action-packed mobile rogue-like game inspired by Brotato, built in Unity and C#.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Mobile
  • Rogue-like

Overview

Slaughter Boy is an action-packed rogue-like game that plunges you into a chaotic world where survival is the only option. Waves of enemies relentlessly pursue you, and the environment constantly shifts, creating a dynamic battlefield filled with danger. As a lone warrior, you must rise against these overwhelming odds, battling foes and unlocking upgrades to become more powerful and ensure your survival.


Drawing inspiration from the fast-paced gameplay of Brotato, Slaughter Boy offers an exciting blend of rapid combat, character progression, and strategic decision-making. Whether you choose to fight alone or experiment with different character builds, the game keeps the action intense and fast, with each wave offering new challenges and enemies to face.


The game features:
• Survival Mode: Endure 20 relentless waves of enemies, each more challenging than the last. Strengthen your skills and gear as you fight to survive until the final wave.
• Shop: Enhance your character by purchasing powerful upgrades, unlocking new abilities, and customizing your playstyle to dominate the battlefield.


Developed with C# and Unity, Slaughter Boy is built for mobile devices, offering a seamless experience on your phone. Created by a dedicated team of six programmers and four artists, the game provides an exciting, fast-paced adventure on the go.

My contributions

Project Leader / Developer:

  • Team Management: Supervised the team, managed schedules, set objectives, and tracked progress to ensure timely delivery.
  • Design Oversight: Directed decisions on gameplay mechanics and interface design to align with the project's vision.

Gameplay & UI Developer:

  • Main Menu: Designed and implemented the game's main menu, ensuring intuitive navigation and functionality.

  • Character Selection: Developed the character selection screen, including detailed displays of each character’s skills and stats.

  • Gameplay Loop: Engineered the core gameplay loop, integrating mechanics for enemy waves, upgrades, and progression.

  • Shop and Upgrades: Created an in-game shop system, allowing players to purchase upgrades and power-ups to enhance characters during gameplay.

  • Tutorial: Built an interactive tutorial to introduce players to controls, objectives, abilities, and survival strategies.

Audio Developer:

  • Sound Effects: Integrated immersive sound effects for combat, environment, and interactions.

  • Music Integration: Implemented dynamic background music and soundtracks in Unity to enhance gameplay atmosphere.
