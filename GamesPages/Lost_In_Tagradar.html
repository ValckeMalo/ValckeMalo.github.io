Lost In Tagradar

An immersive 3D simulation and automation game inspired by Satisfactory, set on an alien planet.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • PC
  • Automation / Simulation

Overview

Lost in Tagradar is an immersive simulation game that plunges you into the heart of an uncharted planet, where survival hinges on your ability to harness the power of automation and industry. Stranded on the alien world of Tagradar, you must explore vast landscapes, gather resources, and build complex factories to unlock the secrets of the planet and secure your place within it.


Whether you're constructing massive industrial complexes or fine-tuning your resource management systems, every step you take brings new challenges and opportunities. The more you optimize, the more you'll unlock—enabling you to push the limits of what's possible in this endless, evolving world.


Inspired by the beloved Satisfactory, Lost in Tagradar offers a rich, open-world experience where exploration and optimization go hand in hand. As you delve deeper into Tagradar’s unknown frontiers, you’ll design intricate production lines, automate processes, and discover innovative solutions to make your factories run more efficiently.


The game features two key elements:
• Exploration Mode: Venture across Tagradar’s vast landscapes, gather resources, and unlock new technologies to improve your factories and systems.
• Optimization Mode: Focus on refining and expanding your automated processes to create the most efficient and effective factories possible.


Developed by a dedicated team of seven programmers and four talented artists, Lost in Tagradar is built with Unity and C# to run smoothly on PC, offering hours of rewarding gameplay as you transform an untamed world into a powerhouse of automation.

Technical environment

  • Engine / Language: Unity / C#
  • Platform: PC (Windows)
  • Key Tech & Algorithms: Mathematical Bezier Curves for Conveyor Belts, Custom Editors, Spatial Partitioning Optimization
  • Team Size: 7 Programmers, 4 Artists

My contributions

Project Leader / Lead Developer:

  • Team Supervision: Managed the overall team, ensuring that tasks, schedules, and objectives were met efficiently.
  • Gameplay Design: Made key decisions regarding gameplay mechanics, interface design, and overall game balance.
  • Planning & Coordination: Oversaw the planning process, ensuring the project progressed smoothly to meet deadlines.

Gameplay Developer:

  • Conveyor Belts: Designed and implemented conveyor belts using a mathematical algorithm to generate constant curves. Managed object transportation, ensuring high performance and visual fluidity.
  • Optimization: Focused on optimizing game processes, reducing CPU/GPU costs, and maintaining high framerates in large scenes containing hundreds of active objects.

Tools Developer:

  • Creation Tools: Developed 4 dedicated Unity custom editor tools to streamline the creation and integration of objects, milestones, crafting recipes, and building structures.
  • Graphic Brush Tool: Designed and developed a custom brush tool for artists, allowing them to paint and generate game objects directly onto the terrain.

Sound Developer:

  • Audio Integration: Integrated sound effects and music into Unity, enhancing atmospheric spatialization and feedback.
