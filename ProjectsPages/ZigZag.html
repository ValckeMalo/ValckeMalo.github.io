GAME PROTOTYPE · SCHOOL PROJECT

ZigZag

A short prototype created in approximately three hours.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Rapid Prototyping
  • Procedural Path

Overview

In another fast-paced school exercise, the challenge was to recreate the core gameplay of ZigZag in Unity within a three-hour time limit. The project emphasized agility, precision, and the ability to distill a game into its essential mechanics.


The recreated game included:
• A zigzagging player-controlled ball, dynamically moving forward along a narrow path.
• Responsive input to change direction at each corner, challenging timing and reflexes.
• A system of procedurally generated paths, ensuring the gameplay remains engaging and unpredictable.
The objective was to capture the essence of ZigZag with a focus on smooth controls and infinite playability.


This project demonstrated the importance of prioritizing core gameplay elements under time constraints. I refined my skills in procedural generation and honed my understanding of Unity’s input handling to deliver a polished and responsive experience.

Technical environment

  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Core Focus: Procedural Path Generation, Input Management, Direction Switching Physics

My contributions

In this time-constrained project, I worked on:


  • Movement & Controls: Ball movement and direction switching, ensuring responsive and satisfying controls for the player.

  • Path Generation: Path generation, creating an endless sequence of randomized turns while maintaining a playable difficulty curve.

  • Collision Mechanics: Implementing collision mechanics, adding fail states when the ball falls off the path.
