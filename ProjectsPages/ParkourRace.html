GAME PROTOTYPE · SOLO PROJECT

Parkour Race

A platformer game project using Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints.

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints
  • Platformer
  • Physics

Overview

Over the course of several weeks, I embarked on a project to learn Unreal Engine 5 by recreating the core gameplay of Fall Guys in a solo effort. This exercise was designed to explore the engine’s capabilities while building a fun and dynamic gameplay experience inspired by the chaotic charm of the original game.


The project focused on delivering key elements of Fall Guys, including:
• Trap and obstacle mechanics, such as spinning bars, falling platforms, and moving blocks, to challenge the player.
• A player-controlled character with physics-based movement and animations, ensuring a playful, responsive feel.
• Multiple maps, each with unique layouts and challenges, to keep gameplay fresh and engaging.


This project allowed me to dive deep into Unreal Engine 5, gaining hands-on experience with Blueprints, physics systems, and level design. By recreating the energetic and unpredictable gameplay of Fall Guys, I also improved my ability to balance mechanics for fun and fairness.

Technical environment

  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
  • Scripting: Blueprints
  • Core Focus: Physics-based Character Movement, Trap Mechanics, Level Design

My contributions

As the sole developer on this project, I handled all development aspects:


  • Obstacle Systems: Designed and implemented trap and obstacle systems, ensuring they interacted smoothly with player physics.

  • Character & Physics: Built a player character system with movement, jumping, and ragdoll-like physics to capture the chaotic fun of Fall Guys.

  • Level Design: Created maps with distinct challenges, leveraging Unreal Engine 5’s powerful level design tools to make environments visually and mechanically engaging.
