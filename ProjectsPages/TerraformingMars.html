Overview

Terraforming Mars is the acclaimed digital board game adaptation of the famous strategy masterpiece. Players take the role of corporations working together or competing to terraform the red planet by raising temperature, creating oxygen, and covering the surface with oceans. Corporations invest in ambitious projects, manage resources, and deploy cards to make Mars habitable.


The Colonies expansion (DLC) expands the experience by allowing players to trade with outer solar system settlements, opening new strategic pathways, introducing new corporations, cards, and specialized trade fleets to master the economy beyond Mars.
