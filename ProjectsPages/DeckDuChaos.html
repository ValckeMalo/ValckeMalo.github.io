Le Donjon de Naheulbeuk: Le Deck du Chaos

A rogue-like deckbuilder RPG set in the iconic universe of Le Donjon de Naheulbeuk.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • PC
  • Deckbuilder RPG

Overview

Le Donjon de Naheulbeuk: Le Deck du Chaos is an exciting rogue-like deckbuilder RPG that plunges you straight back into the chaotic and humorous universe created by John Lang (Pen of Chaos). Developed by Artefacts Studio for PC and Steam Deck, the game offers high replayability with randomly generated rooms filled with quirky monsters, absurd situations, and legendary loot.


As you embark on successive expeditions deep into the dungeon, you'll manage your adventuring party, build powerful decks of cards, make critical tactical choices, and face the inevitable sudden deaths with the signature humor of the saga.

Technical environment

  • Engine: Unity
  • Language: C#
  • Platform: PC / Steam Deck
  • Studio: Artefacts Studio

My contributions

As a developer on the project, my work focused on three key areas:


  • Tools Development: Created robust editor tools and systems to streamline internal workflows and assist content creators and designers.

  • User Interface (UI): Designed and implemented responsive, immersive UI screens and menus adapted to deckbuilding mechanics and inventory management.

  • Gameplay Programming: Implemented core gameplay loops, card interactions, and tactical encounter logic to bring the rogue-like mechanics to life.
